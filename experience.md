## Fieldwork & observing systems

### Underwater glider piloting
**May 2023 – present · SECOORA project**

Optimize waypoint navigation in dynamic ocean conditions, monitor glider behavior, and provide shore-based support for deployment and recovery. Work also includes correcting salinity data from the G1 glider’s non-pumped CTD sensor.

### R/V Sally Ride
**May 27 – June 12, 2023 · California coast · Upwelling dynamics**

CTD operations and drifter deployment.

### High-frequency radar field maintenance
**March 12 – 17, 2023 · Outer Banks, North Carolina**

Radar repair and reinstallation at the HATY, DUCK, and JENN sites.

### R/V Neil Armstrong
**January 2 – 12, 2023 · Cape Hatteras · NASA SWOT calibration/validation project**

CPIES deployment.

### R/V NARA
**March 2019**

Operated CTD instruments and trained undergraduate students in their use.
