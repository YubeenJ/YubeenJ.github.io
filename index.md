## I’m Yubeen, a physical oceanographer.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Marine Sciences at the **University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill**, working with Dr. Harvey Seim.

My research focuses on the dynamics and long-term variability of the Gulf Stream near Cape Hatteras. I use ocean observations to investigate how currents respond to atmospheric forcing and topography, and what maintains their paths before separation.

I am particularly interested in combining in situ measurements and satellite remote sensing to understand the physical processes driving ocean variability.
