## Current research: Gulf Stream dynamics {#gulf-stream-title}

**How do winds and topography shape a strong western boundary current before it separates from the coast?**

Since joining UNC Chapel Hill in August 2022, I have focused on the Gulf Stream near Cape Hatteras. I use long-term high-frequency radar surface-current observations to study changes in its path and current structure. My doctoral work includes the following research questions:

- **Measuring variability:** How can Gulf Stream parameters be extracted and evaluated consistently from long-term HF radar observations?
- **Atmospheric forcing:** What characterizes equatorward shift events, and how are these events related to atmospheric forcing?
- **Topographic interactions:** How does the Gulf Stream recover from topographic perturbations and maintain path coherence before separation?

## Surface & subsurface warming {#warming-title}

**Why can surface warming slow while the subsurface ocean continues to warm?**

My East/Japan Sea warming research began during my master's studies, continued through my associate-researcher appointment at Pusan National University, and was published in 2022. Using ocean reanalysis, evaluated against observations and other reanalyses, we examined differences between surface and subsurface temperature changes. The study connects the 2000–2014 surface warming slowdown and continued subsurface warming with changes in winds, vertical circulation, and the East Korea Warm Current.

## Reconstructing the ocean interior {#ocean-interior-title}

**How much can surface measurements tell us about the ocean below?**

During my master's studies, I investigated the connection between surface satellite measurements and three-dimensional ocean temperature. In our 2019 study, we combined sea-surface temperature, sea-level, and wind-stress anomalies with Argo temperature fields using multiple linear regression. The reconstructed temperature fields extend the view of upper-ocean structure and allow mixed-layer depth to be examined across seasons.

## Mesoscale eddies & boundary currents {#eddies-title}

**How do eddies interact with currents, and how well do their surface signatures describe their structure?**

In my 2019 first-author study, I used satellite altimeter measurements to examine interactions between mesoscale eddies and the Kuroshio Current. This work established an interest in how eddies and strong boundary currents interact, connecting my earlier remote-sensing studies with my current research on Gulf Stream variability.
